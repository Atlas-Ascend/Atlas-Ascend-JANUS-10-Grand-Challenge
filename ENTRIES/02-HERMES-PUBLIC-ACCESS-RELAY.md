# Entry 02 — HERMES / Public Access Relay

**Repo to create:** `Atlas-Ascend/HERMES-Public-Access-Relay`  
**Competition:** Build, Ship, Shape: Amazon Developer Hackathon  
**Selected track:** Alexa+ first; Fire TV reserve  
**Prize:** $138,000 cash across competition tracks  
**Deadline:** Oct 23, 2026 12:00 PM PDT  
**Priority:** P3

## Product Thesis

A conversational public-access relay that converts complex public information and digital-service workflows into accessible, actionable interactions for people who cannot or do not want to navigate conventional web portals.

## Judge-Visible Transformation

`spoken need → intent/context → verified service knowledge → guided action → confirmation / next step`

## Why HERMES

The product is not a generic voice assistant. HERMES is a relay between people and fragmented service systems. Its value is accessibility, context preservation, source visibility, and deterministic handoff when automation cannot legitimately complete the task.

## Sponsor-Tech Contract

Alexa+ must be part of the primary interaction path. If Fire TV proves a stronger competition lane, preserve the HERMES domain and shift only the presentation surface.

## MVP Proof

Demonstrate three different user needs crossing the same relay substrate—for example service discovery, eligibility/document preparation, and status/next-step navigation—with explicit source attribution and a safe human handoff when confidence is insufficient.

## Win Characteristics

- immediately understandable public-access problem;
- voice UX that genuinely removes friction;
- strong accessibility considerations;
- no fabricated government/service actions;
- evidence-backed information;
- useful failure/handoff behavior;
- polished Amazon-device-native experience.
