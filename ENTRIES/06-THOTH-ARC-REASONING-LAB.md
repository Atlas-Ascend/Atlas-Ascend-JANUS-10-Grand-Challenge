## Product / Research Thesis

An auditable abstraction-reasoning laboratory that treats ARC tasks as iterative hypothesis formation, transformation induction, falsification and repair rather than opaque one-shot answer generation.

## Research Transformation

`task examples → object/relationship representation → hypothesis candidates → executable transformations → falsification → confidence/evidence ledger → answer`

## Competition Contract

This entry is research-first. The paper, reproducible notebook/project and required ARC competition linkage are the product. Claims must be backed by experiments, baselines, ablations and documented failure cases.

## Core Research Questions

- Which representations improve abstraction transfer?
- When does explicit hypothesis search outperform direct generation?
- Can a verifier reliably reject attractive but wrong transformations?
- Which task families benefit from tool/program induction?
- How can reasoning traces be made useful for scientific debugging without pretending they are ground-truth cognition?

## MVP Proof

A reproducible evaluation harness that solves a meaningful task subset, compares at least two reasoning strategies, logs hypothesis/evaluation trajectories and produces quantitative + qualitative failure analysis.

## Win Characteristics

- novel but testable thesis;
- rigorous experimental design;
- strong baselines/ablations;
- reproducible code and data pipeline;
- useful negative results;
- clear scientific writing;
- no inflated AGI claims.
