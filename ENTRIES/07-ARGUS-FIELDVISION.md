# Entry 07 — ARGUS / FieldVision

**Repo to create:** `Atlas-Ascend/ARGUS-FieldVision`  
**Competition:** OpenCV AI Competition 2026, powered by AWS  
**Prize:** $20,250 cash advertised  
**Deadline:** Oct 27, 2026 — official portal time controls  
**Priority:** P3

## Product Thesis

An agentic field-vision system that turns camera or image evidence into grounded observations, uncertainty-aware decisions, bounded actions and proof receipts.

## Judge-Visible Transformation

`visual scene → OpenCV perception → structured evidence → contextual reasoning → action/recommendation → visual + machine-readable receipt`

## Sponsor-Tech Contract

OpenCV 5 must perform substantive visual work. AWS must materially support an inference, event, storage, orchestration or verification component. Neither dependency may be decorative.

## MVP Proof

Present a field inspection scenario with multiple visual conditions. ARGUS detects relevant objects/states, localizes evidence, reasons over defined rules/context, explicitly flags low-confidence conditions, and produces an auditable inspection/action packet.

## Safety

No face-identification requirement. Avoid making unsupported high-stakes judgments from appearance. Preserve source imagery provenance and confidence metadata.

## Win Characteristics

- technically impressive OpenCV use;
- visible evidence overlays;
- useful agentic decision path;
- strong real-world operational story;
- robust uncertainty handling;
- AWS integration proof;
- reproducible benchmark/demo set.
