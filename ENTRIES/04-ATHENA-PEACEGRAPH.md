# Entry 04 — ATHENA / PeaceGraph

**Repo to create:** `Atlas-Ascend/ATHENA-PeaceGraph`  
**Competition:** RevenueCat Shipaton 2026  
**Primary award target:** RevenueCat Peace Prize  
**Advertised competition cash pool:** $740,000  
**Deadline:** Sep 30, 2026 11:45 PM PDT  
**Priority:** P2

## Product Thesis

A mobile conflict-intelligence system that converts complex multi-party disputes into an auditable graph of actors, claims, evidence, needs, constraints, harms, shared interests and candidate resolution paths.

## Judge-Visible Transformation

`conflict narrative → structured PeaceGraph → evidence/uncertainty separation → shared-interest surface → candidate resolutions → trade-off comparison → auditable plan`

## RevenueCat Fit

ATHENA must be a **brand-new eligible mobile app** shipped within the competition window. RevenueCat must power a legitimate monetization path such as premium private case spaces, advanced simulations, organization workspaces or another competition-compliant feature. Monetization cannot compromise access to basic safety/help content.

## MVP Proof

User enters a realistic multi-party conflict. ATHENA extracts perspectives without pretending disputed claims are facts, identifies agreement/disagreement surfaces, produces at least three candidate pathways, exposes trade-offs and uncertainty, and exports a transparent resolution packet.

## Safety / Authority

ATHENA is decision support, not coercive arbitration. It must not impersonate legal authority, fabricate evidence or silently rank human groups by worth. High-stakes recommendations must surface assumptions and support human review.

## Win Characteristics

- direct alignment with the Peace Prize category;
- exceptional mobile interaction design;
- evidence graph visibly improves conflict reasoning;
- multiple perspectives remain inspectable;
- clear before/after outcome;
- RevenueCat integration is real and store release is verifiable;
- emotionally serious without becoming sentimental or vague.
